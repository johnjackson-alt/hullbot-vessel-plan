The sides and flat bottom split at midships, 87 m from the aft perpendicular (about Fr 109). The bow zone starts at the forward draft marks (Fr 210) and the stern zone at the stern draft marks (Fr 20). The sides run from the 13.3 m max-draft waterline down to the flat bottom, including the turn of bilge. The rudder and propeller are niche-area zones of their own. Hover or tap a zone to see it.

Deployment points 1–4 Network set-up Control sites Battery charging

Points 1 and 3 sit mid-zone forward (starboard, port). Points 2 and 4 are aft, in line with the stern draft marks. Hullbot is lowered over the rail by hand. There are two control sites: aft, beside the superstructure on the starboard walkway under the liferaft rack (shared with charging), and forward, just aft of the raised forecastle. The tether is paid out at each deployment point. Network as per the Tele-operations Overview: the network zone at the starboard quarter of the aft mooring deck holds the Peplink BR1 and both Starlink Minis (green ring: clear sky above 25°, amber: blocked), with Wi-Fi in blue to the field laptops. Field upload needs at least 25 Mbps. One fleet technician works each deployment point. People, kit and the H6.3 are at true scale; hover any item for what it does. On a tanker the cargo deck is a hazardous area: site positions, charging and any non-Ex equipment need the ship's permit to work and the master's agreement.
